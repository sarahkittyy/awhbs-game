# Audio credits

All files are real recordings/rips downloaded from the web, then converted with ffmpeg
(Ogg Vorbis, mono, 44.1 kHz, leading silence trimmed, SFX peak-normalized to about -1 dBFS,
music loudness-normalized to about -14 LUFS). Nothing was synthesized.

Ownership / license notes:
- **Halo**: sound effects (C) Microsoft / Bungie / 343 Industries. Fan rips, used here only in a
  personal, non-commercial fan project. Not redistributable.
- **OMORI**: sound effects and music (C) OMOCAT, LLC. Fan rips, used here only in a personal,
  non-commercial fan project. Not redistributable.
- **Kenney**: "Impact Sounds" pack by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 (public domain).
